Privacy Policy

App Name: Easy Magnifier - Reading Glass & Freeze (쉬운 돋보기)
Developer: muhons
Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Summary: This app does not require user registration or account login. We strictly respect your privacy. The camera permission is requested solely to provide the real-time magnifying viewfinder and screen freeze functions locally on your device. We do NOT record, store, or transmit your camera feed or images to any external servers. Built-in flashlight (torch) control is processed strictly on your hardware. Non-personally identifiable device information may be automatically processed via official Google SDKs solely to display top banner advertisements (Google AdMob).

1. Camera Access and Information We Collect

Easy Magnifier operates purely on your device without operating any backend servers. It accesses only the following device features locally:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly to stream the live camera preview onto your screen so you can zoom in and read small text. When you use the "Freeze" function, the captured frame is held temporarily in your device's memory for reading and is never uploaded or transmitted outside your smartphone.
  • Flashlight / Torch Hardware: Used solely to turn the camera LED flash ON and OFF when reading in dark environments or poorly lit menus.
  • Haptic Feedback (Vibration): Used solely to provide subtle tactile vibration when double-tapping to freeze or tapping control buttons.
  • Local Preferences (SharedPreferences): Stores your cumulative freeze usage count (for in-app review criteria) and first-time usage guide status strictly on your phone. This local data is immediately and permanently erased when the app is uninstalled.

However, third-party SDKs integrated into the app (Google AdMob and Google Play In-App Review) may automatically process certain non-personally identifiable information:

  • Collected Data: Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, OS version, network operator, IP address (for coarse geographic location), ad impressions, and click telemetry.
  • Method: Automatically collected via the official Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Services.

2. How We Use Collected Information

The non-identifiable technical information collected by Google SDKs is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Delivering top banner advertisements and preventing invalid click activity or fraudulent impressions.
  • Providing the official Google Play In-App Review prompt and monitoring app stability to prevent crashes.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with official Google services for monetization and app reliability. For details on how Google processes your data, please review their official privacy documentation:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store any personal data or photos on external servers. All temporary freeze frames and user settings reside exclusively in volatile device memory or private app storage and are completely deleted when the app is closed, cleared, or uninstalled.

5. User Rights and Opt-Out Options

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time via your Android settings:

  • Android: Settings ➔ Security & Privacy ➔ Privacy ➔ Ads ➔ [Reset advertising ID] or [Delete advertising ID].

6. Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Developer: muhons
  • Email: muhons@gmail.com
