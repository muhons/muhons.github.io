Privacy Policy

App Name: Easy Converter (쉬운 단위 변환기)
Developer: muhons
Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Summary: Easy Converter does not require account registration, login, or any dangerous runtime permissions (such as camera, location, contacts, microphone, or storage). We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). All unit conversions and preferences are processed strictly in real-time on your device. Non-personally identifiable device information may be automatically processed via official Google SDKs solely to serve advertisements (Google AdMob).

1. Information We Collect and Hardware Access

This application does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Dangerous Permissions: We do not request access to contacts, photos, storage, microphone, or camera.
  • Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`): Used solely to fetch publicly available daily European Central Bank (ECB) exchange rates and to serve Google AdMob advertisements.
  • Local Preferences (`SharedPreferences`): Stores the user's last selected base units and session cooldown flags locally on the device. All preferences are completely removed upon app uninstallation.

2. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements for free service maintenance. Google may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Coarse IP-based location and network status
  • Device model, OS version, and ad interaction logs

For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

The app does not operate external databases or servers. All local configuration data stored on your device is deleted immediately when the application is uninstalled.
