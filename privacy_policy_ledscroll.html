Privacy Policy

App Name: Easy LED
Developer: muhons
Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Summary: This app does not require account registration or login. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, or contacts. Non-personally identifiable device information may be automatically processed via official Google SDKs solely to serve advertisements (Google AdMob) and facilitate app ratings (Google Play In-App Review).

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Collected

The app does not operate any backend servers. All your customizable LED banner settings (text, colors, speed, fonts) are stored locally on your device.

However, third-party SDKs integrated into the app may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information:

  • Collected Data: Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, OS version, network state, IP address (for coarse geographic location), ad impressions, and click logs.
  • Method: Automatically collected through the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google Play Services.

2. How We Use Collected Information

The non-identifiable information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Delivering banner and interstitial advertisements and preventing fraudulent impressions or clicks.
  • Analyzing app stability and crashes to improve performance.
  • Supporting Google Play In-App Review flows.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use official Google services for monetization and analytics. For details on how Google processes your data, please refer to their policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your data on external servers. All local banner data stored on your device is immediately and permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app storage in your device settings.

5. User Rights and Opt-Out Options

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings ➔ Security & Privacy ➔ Privacy ➔ Ads ➔ [Reset advertising ID] or [Delete advertising ID].

6. Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Developer: muhons
  • Email: muhons@gmail.com
