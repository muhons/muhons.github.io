Privacy Policy

App Name: Bubble Level / Easy Bubble Level (쉬운 수평기)
Developer: muhons
Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Summary: This app does not require account registration, login, or any dangerous runtime permissions (such as camera, location, contacts, or storage). We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII). Built-in motion sensors (gravity and accelerometer) are processed strictly in real time on your device to display surface inclination and are never transmitted externally. Non-personally identifiable device information may be automatically processed via official Google SDKs solely to serve banner advertisements (Google AdMob).

1. Hardware Access and Information We Collect

The app does not operate any backend servers and accesses only the following device features locally:

  • Gravity & Accelerometer Sensors: Uses the standard Android Sensor API (Sensor.TYPE_GRAVITY and Sensor.TYPE_ACCELEROMETER) solely to calculate horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) tilt angles in real time on your screen. Sensor readings are never recorded or sent outside your device.
  • Vibration (Haptic Feedback): Used solely to provide gentle haptic vibration feedback when the surface reaches 0.0° level or when buttons are tapped.
  • Local Preferences (SharedPreferences): Stores your 0° calibration offset and vibration ON/OFF setting strictly on your device. This local data is automatically and permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

However, third-party SDKs integrated into the app (Google AdMob and Google Play In-App Review) may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information:

  • Collected Data: Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, OS version, network state, IP address (for coarse geographic location), ad impressions, and click logs.
  • Method: Automatically collected through the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google Play Services.

2. How We Use Collected Information

The non-identifiable information collected by Google SDKs is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Delivering top banner advertisements and preventing fraudulent impressions or invalid clicks.
  • Providing the official Google Play In-App Review dialog and analyzing app stability/crashes to improve service reliability.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use official Google services for monetization and stability. For details on how Google processes your data, please refer to their official policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store any personal or user data on external servers. All user preferences (such as 0° calibration values) are stored only in your device's local app storage and are immediately erased when the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared.

5. User Rights and Opt-Out Options

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings ➔ Security & Privacy ➔ Privacy ➔ Ads ➔ [Reset advertising ID] or [Delete advertising ID].

6. Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Developer: muhons
  • Email: muhons@gmail.com
